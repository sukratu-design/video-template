## Source and accuracy notes
- Started from a Grok draft supplied by the user (30-40 s). Expanded to ~75 s to match the painted-film format and to answer the actual question (why ~20 days?).
- **Festival gap vs the epic.** Valmiki's Yuddha Kanda gives no day count matching Dussehra (Ashvin shukla dashami) to Diwali (Kartik amavasya), and does not mention lamps or Diwali. The film says outright that the ~20-day count is later tradition (beat 2) and that the lamps are "tradition says" (beat 10). The moon strip shows the calendar gap, not the epic.
- **Kept from Valmiki:** Rama urges a reluctant Vibhishana to perform Ravana's rites ("enmity ends with death"); Vibhishana's consecration (Rama had promised him Lanka when he came over) comes before Sita is brought; then the fire ordeal, after which Agni returns Sita unharmed and declares her pure; Vibhishana offers the Pushpaka; the flight stops at Kishkindha and at Bharadwaja's ashram, from where Hanuman is sent ahead to Bharata; Bharata, who ruled from Nandigram with Rama's sandals on the throne, returns them at the reunion as the fourteen years end.
- **Removed from the draft:** "to prove her purity" (kept the event and its outcome, neutrally).
- **Not said:** Dussehra/Vijayadashami also marks Durga's victory over Mahishasura in eastern India; left out for length (user chose the default).

## Changes from the approved table
- Length 77.5 s instead of ~70 s: the measured takes were longer than estimated and every line was kept at tempo 1.0 rather than sped up.
- Beat 8 adds a small "Nandigram" label under the throne (where Bharata ruled from, per Valmiki).
- Beat 9: the sandals settle onto the arriving footprints (rather than two sets of footprints meeting).
